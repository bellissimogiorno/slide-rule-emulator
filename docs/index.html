Programme JSON

Custom expressions use a restricted mathematical language; they cannot execute JavaScript. Arithmetic, powers, parentheses and approved Math functions are supported. For an expression scale, pos receives x and returns 0..1; value receives p and returns the scale value. Built-in programmable types also include root, loglog, foldedLog, foldedInverse, trigonometric and hyperbolic mappings, and pythagorean.

Pickett square and cube roots

Load Pickett N3 or N4 and select Front or Both sides. The square-root branches meet one common line. The cube-root bank has an unruled upper branch and a back-to-back middle/lower pair. Both number modes use this construction. Previously saved layouts are retained; use Load preset to adopt the current arrangement.

At D = 6.4, the cube-root branches read about 1.85664, 4 and 8.61774, for 6.4, 64 and 640 respectively. For a positive number N = m × 10k, with 1 ≤ m < 10, set D to m. For an order-n root, choose branch 1 plus the nonnegative remainder of k divided by n, then multiply its reading by 10floor(k/n). This works for negative exponents too. The gutter digit-count reminders apply to positive integers.

The root branches have independently positioned round-number graduations; they are not reciprocal log-log pairs. All branches increase to the right and use black ink. In the library, PICKETT_SQRT and PICKETT_CBRT add complete movable banks; the generic roots, fifth roots and Faber-Castell W scales remain independent.

Chemical scales (Hemmi 257 / Flying Fish 1017)

Load Hemmi 257 for its chemical back, or Flying Fish 1017 for its chemical front. Choose Both sides for cross-face work. These are different presets, with their own scientific faces and photographed row arrangements. The calibrations are modern educational models, not a photographic or certified historical reproduction.

Ch A-M / N-Z: 52 element and 18 group/reference marks align with fixed D on the scientific face. Underlined symbols use D × 1, plain symbols D × 10, overlined symbols D × 100. Hover a visible symbol for its value. Readouts identify the nearest mark; they do not interpolate an atomic weight. Representative CIAAW 2024 values are used; Ra[226] is explicitly a mass-number convention.

M1 / Xw and M2 / X (Flying Fish: Mz / Xz and M / X): each row packs a mass setting on its left and a fraction scale on its right. Align M2 with M1, keeping the same decimal rescaling for both. For a 20% by mass ammonium-sulphate solution, align M2 = 1.8 under M1 = 13.21, set the cursor to Xw = 0.2, and read X ≈ 0.033. Xw and X are fractions, not percentages. Density substitution gives volume fractions only under the additive-volume assumption.

Temperature: T F and T C (plus T K on the Hemmi) convert a common 100-1000 K range. They share the slide: conversion between them works at any slide setting. Reset the slide to align them with C/D on the other face.

Pressure: the common six-decade coordinate covers 0.001-1000 kgf/cm² absolute. The paired row reads mmHg absolute below one atmosphere and atm above it. The additional Hemmi row reads inHg vacuum below one atmosphere and psi gauge above it. Gauge/vacuum zero uses 101325 Pa, not the ambient pressure at your location. Boundary values of the two segments refer to the same absolute pressure.

tw: read equilibrium temperature against the fixed pressure rows, independently of the slide. IAPWS correlations cover ice below the triple point and liquid-water/steam above it, from -20 °C to the critical point at 373.946 °C. No steam saturation state exists above that endpoint.

F(t): air is on the left, CO2 on the right. Align the initial gas temperature on F(t) to the initial absolute pressure on P; move the cursor to the final pressure and read the final temperature on the same gas segment. These are reversible, fixed-composition ideal-gas calculations using NIST heat-capacity fits, not compressor-design or real-gas predictions. Air means 79% N2 / 21% O2, valid here from 200-1000 K; CO2 covers 298.15-900 K. An off-scale result is not extrapolated.

Flying Fish 1017 notes

The chemical face includes the near-unity LnI−2 and Ln−2 ranges omitted from the Hemmi layout. The back has LnI−1, LnI0, LnI1, A1; moving lg, T/cot, S/cos, CI, C; and fixed D, Ln1, Ln0, Ln−1. Ln and LnI are exponential log-log scales, not the ordinary logarithm.

The shared chemical functions use the same documented modern mass, pressure, water and ideal-gas models as the Hemmi study. They do not claim identical historical engraving. Original scale extensions, the full historical symbol repertoire and unverified gauge constants are not reproduced. See docs/FLYING_FISH_1017.md in the source package for the photographic evidence and exact mapping convention.

How to use it

Drag the middle slide to set the alignment. Drag the glass cursor to read all scales at the hairline. On a classic multiplication setup, align C 1 over a D number, then read products by moving the cursor to another C number and reading D.

Choose Both sides to see the faces together. Their slide and cursor positions stay linked. In that view, Edit face selects which layout you are changing. The two faces are shown upright, not mirrored.

Full-width workspace: the instrument always comes first, across the available page width. Scale layout and JSON controls are below it; their own columns adapt to the screen. The Scale layout and Back to rule links jump between the two, and Focus rule hides the editor without changing the graduation width.

Expanded roots: write the radicand as N = m × 10^k, with 1 ≤ m < 10. For an n-th root, choose branch (k mod n) + 1, put the cursor at m on D, and read the root mantissa on that branch. Example: 32 = 3.2 × 10^1, so 5R2 reads 2 over D = 3.2. A, K, and x⁵ provide the corresponding power scales.

Log-log: LL0 through LL4 cover values above 1; LL00 through LL04 cover values below 1. Pickett paired and Syncro LL± rows use one shared graduation: the black number is above one and the red number immediately below it is its exact reciprocal. The N3 rows are natural-exponential; the N4 rows use Pickett's base-10 geometry.

Compact scale numbering: primary numerals are tucked beside their long ticks. When a label cannot fit cleanly to the right, suitable multi-digit labels are divided around the tick. Turn the option off to restore centred-above-tick numbering. A custom scale can opt out with "compactLabels": false.

Trig and hyperbolic: S, COS, T1, T2, ST, and P cover circular functions. SH1, SH2, TH, H1, H2, and CH provide split hyperbolic-function ranges. Load the Trigonometric or Hyperbolic preset for a less crowded working face.
