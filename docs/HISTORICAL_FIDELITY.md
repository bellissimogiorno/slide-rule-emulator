# Historical fidelity and appropriate use

These are programmable, formula-driven digital instruments. Their names and appearance acknowledge historical products without asserting endorsement, dimensional photographic fidelity or complete operational calibration.

| Preset group | Intended scope | Remaining qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faber-Castell 2/83N | Scale-order/layout study, W root arrangement, colour and formula hierarchy | Front circle-area and folded 360 cursor marks are included; additional original lines remain unresolved. See [cursors](CURSORS_FABER_ARISTO.md) |
| Pickett N3 / N4 | Photographed back-to-back root banks and genuinely shared reciprocal LL graduations | Root branches retain independent tick positions; N3 natural and N4 base-10 LL geometry are distinct. Finite screen engraving is not a scan; see [Pickett roots](PICKETT_ROOTS.md) |
| Thornton AA010 | Standard arithmetic/trigonometric families and paired presentation | Correction rows are normalised mathematical studies, not fully calibrated historical differential scales |
| K&E Deci-Lon | Cream/red layout and scale-family study | Explicitly inspired by the instrument, not a calibrated dimensional replica |
| Pickett Model 14 | General log-log and degree/6400-mil faces, with shared angle ticks | Main-index mathematical reconstruction; no original end extensions or firing tables. See [Pickett 14](PICKETT_14.md) |
| Pickett N16 | Standard scientific front, illustrative electronics reverse | Electronics reverse uses normalised relationships, without the full original decimal-point/unit system |
| Pickett Model 17 | Energy/photon radiation relationships and tested temperature-to-ratio alignment | Educational calculation, not certified radiation metrology; not every manual workflow reproduced |
| Thornton F5100 | Lettered scale arrangement and radiation/wavelength references | Operational coupling and full historical instruction workflow are not established |
| Hemmi 257 | Documented row order and working mixture/temperature/pressure relationships | Modern representative chemical weights, IAPWS ice/steam relation and NIST ideal-gas F(t), not original engraved calibration; see CHEMICAL_SCALES.md |
| Flying Fish 1017 | Photographed chemical/scientific row order, Ln/LnI, T/cot, Mz/Xz and M/X | Uses the documented modern chemical models; original gauge set, end extensions, all reference symbols and exact historic calibrations are not transcribed; see FLYING_FISH_1017.md |
| Laboratory presets | Deliberate digital combinations | Not historical products |

The application includes instrument-specific warnings alongside the presets. A fixed numerical formula can be correct while its placement next to another scale still fails to implement an intended historical operation. New calibration claims require independent worked examples with the actual sliding alignment and units.

Do not use the emulator as the sole basis for safety-critical engineering, radiation protection, financial decisions or other work where errors have substantial consequences. Keep independent calculation checks and portable programme backups.

Historical manuals and photos are research pointers in REFERENCE_REGISTER.md. They are not bundled, and no right to redistribute third-party scans, logos or fonts is asserted.

## Faber-Castell 2/82N

The [2/82N preset](FABER_282N.md) follows the 1962 manufacturer's N-model
manual: 13 front rows and 14 back rows, including sliding S' and K'. It is not
the later 24-row 2/82. S/S' and both tangent ranges have complementary black/red
angle readings; P is reverse-running red. Main mathematical intervals are
implemented, not every end extension, gauge, physical reference table or
auxiliary cursor line.

## Mathema and Aristo hyperbolic presets

See [Mathema 2/84N](MATHEMA.md) and [Aristo 0971 / 0972](ARISTO_HYPERBOLIC.md) for scale-by-scale mathematical conventions, source evidence, and the limits of the reconstruction. These are programmable mathematical instruments with documented scale arrangements. The Mathema includes its calibrated exponent cursor banks, ordinary π conversion hairlines and scale-specific extensions. Their guides distinguish photographed evidence, exact mathematical reconstruction and remaining differences in engraving or hardware.

## Cursor evidence

The [cursor review](CURSORS.md) covers every preset and face. It distinguishes
manufacturer-calibrated auxiliary marks, documented single-line cursors,
representative housing proportions and unresolved physical details. The 2/82N
remains a partial cursor reconstruction; Graphoplex uses the specified manual
variant, and the F5100 back cursor is a digital reference aid.
